# Review the pilot preview

Open **START_PILOT_PREVIEW.html** in Chrome, Edge or another modern browser.
It works offline on your laptop without Python, the venv or a local server.
Copy that HTML file to another computer, or take this entire folder/ZIP.

1. Read **About the study**. This is an English project-review draft, not the
   launched participant survey.
2. Open **Your needs** and try the questionnaire. Try an active preference
   without a target, then supply its target. Try requirements and a budget.
   Practice entries stay only in the current browser page; a reload clears them.
3. Open **Laptop cards**. Browse 51 verified configurations and their 60 dated
   offers, search a model/SKU, or try the filters after completing a profile.
   Check the exact source/offer, price, stock, specifications, delivered OS,
   unknowns and check-date wording. These are working catalogue panels, not
   approved survey assignments. Preferences do not rank the cards in this preview.
4. Try **1–5**, **Skip** and **Unable to judge**. These are temporary practice
   controls; they do not create participant ratings. The eventual participant
   flow will use the approved frozen catalogue and at most fifteen assignments.
5. In **Review & feedback**, write wording/card/layout corrections. Choose
   **Download review feedback** and send the JSON file back in the existing chat.
   Download before closing or reloading. It includes your design notes and
   preview references, without the practice profile, consent or ratings.

No account or internet is needed. Opening a retailer-source link intentionally
opens that external website and does require internet. Do not add participant
names, contact information or actual study responses to the feedback notes.

The existing catalogue approvals and eleven dated second checks are retained.
Ro reports completion of checks within 20–28 October; the unknown individual
dates remain visible. No blanket repeat of completed reviews is requested.
Actual freeze, final participant information/administration details, wording
review and required study approvals remain pending.
