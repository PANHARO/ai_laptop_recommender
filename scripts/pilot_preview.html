About this project

The study concerns laptop suitability for university work. Taking part in the eventual study is voluntary. You may stop, skip a laptop or choose “Unable to judge.”

We ask about age group, university year, budget and laptop needs. No names, phone numbers, email addresses, university names or student IDs are requested.

These are dated retailer offers. Stock may be unavailable, coming soon or not stated. Judge the laptop using the information shown; a card does not guarantee immediate purchase availability or software performance.

Budget and activities

What will you use it for? Select all that apply.
This answer does not automatically set hardware requirements.

Requirements it must meet

A requirement excludes a laptop that does not meet it. If a required specification is unknown on a laptop, it cannot pass that requirement. Choose “Not sure” when you cannot give a value.

The delivered operating system appears on each laptop card. It is not used as an eligibility requirement in this pilot.

Preferences you would like

Importance: 0 = inactive, 1 = very low, 2 = low, 3 = moderate, 4 = high, 5 = very high. A target is required whenever importance is above zero.
